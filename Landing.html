Why it matters

A fire doubles in size every minute it goes unseen.

Most of a factory floor is out of sight most of the time. By the time a heat detector trips or someone smells smoke, the fire already has a head start. SENTRY watches every camera at once and flags the first visible sign — so the response starts in seconds, not minutes.

How it works

Three stages, one direction.

Video flows one way — from the floor, through detection, to the people who need to act.

STAGE 01

Cameras stream

IP cameras across the floor feed the network. The pipeline is proven today on standard cameras over the LAN.

→
STAGE 02

The unit detects

A GPU unit runs fire and smoke detection on every feed at once, confirming smoke over time to rule out steam and dust.

→
STAGE 03

You're alerted

The dashboard raises the alarm, logs the event, and stands ready to export a report — with siren and push alerts next.

Safety positioning

An early-warning layer — not a replacement.

SENTRY buys you seconds by catching fire and smoke visually, the moment they appear. It runs alongside your certified smoke and heat detectors, which remain your code-compliant, fail-safe baseline and keep working if a network or server ever goes down. Layered defense, with redundancy — never a single point of failure for life safety.
